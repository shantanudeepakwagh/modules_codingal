Typography with nature

Nature includes all living and non-living things found around us, such as plants, animals, water, air, and land.
Plants and trees provide oxygen, food, shelter, and many other resources that are important for life.
Animals depend on their surroundings for food, water, protection, and a suitable place to live.

Forests, rivers, mountains, and oceans are important parts of the natural environment.

Protecting nature helps maintain a healthy environment and supports the well-being of people, plants, and animals.
