Ethiopian Cuisine

Ethiopian cuisine is known for its flavorful dishes, spices, and traditional cooking methods. Popular foods include injera, a soft sourdough flatbread, which is often served with stews, vegetables, and meat dishes. Ethiopian meals are commonly enjoyed by sharing food with family and friends.

Ethiopian cuisine includes a variety of delicious dishes and flavorful ingredients. Traditional meals are often served on injera, which is used to scoop up different stews and vegetables.
