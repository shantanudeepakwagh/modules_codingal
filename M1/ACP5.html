Types of Forks - Different Forks and Their Uses

Fork Description
Dinner Fork A dinner fork is a common table utensil used for eating main courses such as vegetables, pasta, rice, and meat.
Salad Fork A salad fork is smaller than a dinner fork and is designed for eating salads, vegetables, and other light foods.
Dessert Fork A dessert fork is a small fork used for eating cakes, pastries, fruits, and other sweet dishes.
Serving Fork A serving fork is larger than a regular fork and is used to serve food from a shared dish onto a plate.
Carving Fork A carving fork has long, strong tines and is used to hold meat securely while it is being carved.
