Facts About Forks

Forks are eating utensils that are commonly used to pick up, hold, and eat food. They usually have a handle and several pointed prongs called tines. Forks have been used for centuries and have evolved into many different designs for different types of food. Today, forks are commonly made from stainless steel, plastic, wood, or other materials.

The fork is one of the most common utensils found around the world. Different types of forks include dinner forks, salad forks, dessert forks, carving forks, and serving forks. Their size and shape can vary depending on their purpose.
