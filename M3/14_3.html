Abbreviations

Abbreviations can be marked using the abbr element:

cdgl was started in 2020.

Blockquotes

Blockquotes are used to display content from another source:

Codingal aims to inspire students to learn and create

From Codingal

For displaying several lines of code, use the pre element:

            codingal is on a
            mission to
            inspire young
            students
